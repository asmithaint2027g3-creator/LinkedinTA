80% of enterprise AI pilots succeed in sandbox environments. Less than 15% ever scale into production.

The pilot stage is easy because it ignores the four hardest enterprise realities: legacy data isolation, deterministic SLAs, sovereign compliance, and compounding inference unit economics.

1. The Synthetic Data Trap: Pilots run on sanitized CSV dumps; production requires streaming, messy, multi-tenant enterprise data.

2. The Single-User Fallacy: A prototype that responds in 4 seconds to one user collapses under enterprise concurrency and multi-region availability.

3. The Governance Chasm: Sandbox demos don't face audit committees, EU AI Act risk tiers, or SOC2 Type II egress scrutiny.

4. The Architecture Mismatch: Wrapping an external API in Python is a weekend hack; building resilient agentic systems of record requires systems-level engineering.

Don't measure AI progress by how many pilots you've launched. Measure progress by how many automated workflows are executing with verifiable SLAs inside core systems.
