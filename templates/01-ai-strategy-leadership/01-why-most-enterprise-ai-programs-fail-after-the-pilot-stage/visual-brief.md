## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on why most enterprise ai programs fail after the pilot stage, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline

- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`AI STRATEGY & LEADERSHIP`) + Topic ID (`#001`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:

  4. The Architecture Mismatch: Wrapping an external API in Python is a weekend hack; building resilient agentic systems of record requires systems-level engineering.
