Experimentation asks: 'Can the model do this?' Operations asks: 'Can our enterprise sustain this at 99.95% uptime and deterministic cost?'

Most organizations run AI like a R&D lab when they need to run it like an industrial manufacturing line.

1. Deterministic SLAs vs Probabilistic Hope: Moving from prompt engineering to rigorous boundary enforcement, unit test suites, and regression guards.

2. Continuous Attestation: Real-time monitoring for model drift, tool failure rates, and latent token thrashing.

3. Unified Infrastructure Control: Managing heterogeneous clusters (H100/H200, Blackwell, edge nodes) through a single telemetry plane.

4. Embedded Forward Deployed Engineers: Bridging data scientists and core enterprise platform teams to deploy directly into production VPCs.

Until AI is governed by the same change management, telemetry, and uptime SLAs as your ERP, it is an experiment, not an enterprise capability.
