# Visual Brief: The Evolution From RPA to Agentic Automation

## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on the evolution from rpa to agentic automation, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline
- **Headline**: THE EVOLUTION OF ENTERPRISE AUTOMATION
- **Sub-headline**: From Brittle Screen Scraping (RPA) to Self-Healing Agentic Operations

## 3. Visual Content Hierarchy
- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`AUTOMATION & OPERATIONS`) + Topic ID (`#048`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:
  1. Generation 1 (Macros & Scripts): Simple deterministic rules executing on single desktop instances.
  2. Generation 2 (RPA & OCR): Brittle screen scraping, high maintenance overhead, zero cognitive reasoning.
  3. Generation 3 (Intelligent Document Processing): Narrow machine learning models extracting structured fields from clean PDFs.
  4. Generation 4 (Autonomous Agentic Systems): Multi-modal understanding, dynamic tool generation, persistent memory, and autonomous exception recovery.
- **Takeaway Rule Banner**: "Don't spend another fiscal year paying maintenance fees on brittle bots. Evolve your operational stack to autonomous agentic systems."
- **Footer**: TrustGrid Engineering Services CTA + trustgrid.ai domain signature
