The biggest risk in enterprise AI is not that autonomous systems make mistakes. The biggest risk is deploying them without knowing how they fail.

Autonomous operations offer 70%+ margin improvements, but they introduce new risks: compounding hallucinations, cascading tool errors, and model drift.

1. The Reward: Straight-through operational velocity, 24/7 autonomous task processing, and massive reduction in back-office unit costs.

2. The Risk: Unconstrained agent loops running up runaway cloud bills or writing bad data across thousands of ERP ledger entries.

3. The Engineering Reality: Production success requires strict circuit breakers, deterministic parameter boundaries, and continuous attestation.

4. The Pragmatic Path: Start with low-consequence internal tasks, establish verifiable verification metrics, and progressively expand the autonomy boundary.

Real operational leadership is neither blind optimism nor defensive paralysis. It is engineering rigorous boundaries that make autonomy safe.
