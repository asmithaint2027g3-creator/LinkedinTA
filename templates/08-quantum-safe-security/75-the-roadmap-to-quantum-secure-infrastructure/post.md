1. Phase 1: Discovery & CBOM Generation (Cataloging all algorithms, keys, certificates, and data assets across the enterprise).

2. Phase 2: Crypto-Agility Engineering (Decoupling cryptographic implementations from core business logic to allow seamless algorithm upgrades).

3. Phase 3: Hybrid Deployment & Validation (Running dual classical and post-quantum keys in staging and pilot production environments).

4. Phase 4: Full Production Cutover & Deprecation (Sunsetting legacy RSA/ECC ciphers and establishing continuous cryptographic attestation).

A structured, phased roadmap turns an existential cryptographic crisis into a manageable, orderly engineering project.
