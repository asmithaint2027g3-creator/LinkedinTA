To provide high-authority visual clarity on how enterprises waste millions in underutilized compute, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`AI INFRASTRUCTURE ENGINEERING`) + Topic ID (`#014`)
