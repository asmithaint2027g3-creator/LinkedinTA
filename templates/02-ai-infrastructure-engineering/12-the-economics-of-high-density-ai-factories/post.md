Traditional data centers were built for 8kW to 15kW racks. Modern AI clusters demand 40kW to 100kW+ per rack.

Trying to spread modern GPU compute across legacy low-density halls explodes optical cabling distances, introduces network latency, and destroys facility PUE.

1. Physical Footprint Compression: High-density 80kW racks reduce required data center square footage by up to 70%.

2. Optical Transit Reduction: Keeping GPU nodes within short-reach copper/active optical cable (AOC) boundaries slashes network transit latency and transceiver costs.

3. Thermal PUE Gains: Moving from legacy air handlers to direct liquid loops reduces cooling overhead from 40% of facility power to under 8%.

4. Capex Amortization: Higher density accelerates time-to-first-token and maximizes intelligence produced per megawatt.

In the era of frontier models, megawatts are the new currency. High-density design is how you maximize intelligence per dollar.
