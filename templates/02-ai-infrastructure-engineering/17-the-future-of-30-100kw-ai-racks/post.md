Five years ago, a 20kW rack was considered high density. In 2026, 40kW to 100kW per rack is the baseline standard for AI factories.

Packing 100kW into a standard 42U footprint requires solving three simultaneous physical challenges: heavy structural floor loading, high-voltage busway distribution, and continuous hydronic cooling loops.

1. Structural Weight Dynamics: Liquid-filled chassis and heavy copper heatsinks pushing rack weights past 3,000 lbs.

2. Power Distribution Transition: Migrating from 208V AC to 415V/480V busbars and 48V DC power shelves to eliminate rectifier losses.

3. Hydronic Loop Redundancy: Dual-loop CDUs (Coolant Distribution Units) ensuring zero disruption during thermal pump maintenance.

4. Facility Footprint Optimization: Achieving 5x compute capacity within existing building envelopes.

The future of AI is physical. The companies that master thermal and electrical density will lead the intelligence economy.
