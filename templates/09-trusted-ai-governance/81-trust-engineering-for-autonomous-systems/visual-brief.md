## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on trust engineering for autonomous systems, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline
- **Headline**: THE TRUST ENGINEERING ARCHITECTURE
- **Sub-headline**: Input Bounds, Deterministic Tool Interception, Grounding Checks, and Rollback Rails

## 3. Visual Content Hierarchy
- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`TRUSTED AI & GOVERNANCE`) + Topic ID (`#081`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:
