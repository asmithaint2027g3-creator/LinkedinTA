## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on explainable ai is becoming a regulatory requirement, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline
- **Headline**: EXPLAINABLE AI REGULATORY ARCHITECTURE

## 3. Visual Content Hierarchy
- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`TRUSTED AI & GOVERNANCE`) + Topic ID (`#076`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:
  1. The End of the Black Box: Regulators no longer tolerate uninterpretable deep learning models in credit scoring, claims adjudication, or healthcare triage.
  2. Feature Attribution Methods: Integrating SHAP (Shapley Additive exPlanations) and LIME directly into inference serving to quantify exact decision drivers.
