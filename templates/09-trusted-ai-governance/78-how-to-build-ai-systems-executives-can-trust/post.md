Executives do not distrust AI because they are anti-technology. They distrust AI because they have been burned by unfulfilled promises and unexplainable errors.

Trust is not built through charismatic demos. Trust is earned through deterministic bounds, transparent failure modes, and mathematical verification.

1. 1. Deterministic Bounds over Probabilistic Hope: Wrapping LLMs in rigid boundary validation logic that prevents out-of-spec actions.

2. 2. Explicit Uncertainty Calibration: Teaching models to say 'I have low confidence in this answer; routing to human review' rather than hallucinating with confidence.

3. 3. Radical Operational Transparency: Giving business leaders real-time visibility into why decisions were made and which tools were invoked.

4. 4. Starting with Low-Risk, High-Repetition Work: Proving reliability on high-volume, low-consequence tasks before expanding to mission-critical operations.

Trust is an engineering outcome, not a marketing message. Build systems that prove their reliability every single day.
