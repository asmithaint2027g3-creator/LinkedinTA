Traditional firewalls look for malformed packets and malicious binaries. They are blind to a perfectly grammatical English sentence that hijacks your enterprise AI.

The integration of LLMs and autonomous agents into corporate data lakes and enterprise APIs creates an unprecedented attack surface that bypasses legacy security stacks.

1. 1. Indirect Prompt Injection: An attacker embeds invisible instructions inside a vendor invoice or public web page, causing an agent to exfiltrate private client records.

2. 2. Training Data Poisoning: Injecting subtly biased or backdoored records into fine-tuning datasets to compromise model behavior under specific triggers.

3. 3. Agent Privilege Escalation: Agents granted broad corporate API tokens inadvertently executing destructive database mutations on behalf of unauthorized users.

4. 4. The Security Mandate: Transitioning to Zero-Trust AI Architecture with continuous input sanitation, egress filtering, and ephemeral scoped IAM.

In the era of autonomous AI, natural language is executable code. If you do not secure the language runtime, you do not have security.
