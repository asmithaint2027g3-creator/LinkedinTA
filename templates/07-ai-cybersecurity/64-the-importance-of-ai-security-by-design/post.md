Security added at the end of an AI project is not security. It is expensive camouflage.

When security teams are brought in after an AI platform is built, their only choices are to delay launch by 6 months or rubber-stamp dangerous architectural compromises.

1. Threat Modeling at Ideation: Identifying data flow vulnerabilities, prompt injection vectors, and unauthorized tool escalations during initial design sprints.

2. Automated CI/CD Red-Teaming: Continuous automated adversarial attacks testing model boundaries for hallucinations and jailbreaks on every commit.

3. Data Poisoning Audits: Verifying the cryptographic integrity and provenance of all training datasets and embedding vector indices.

4. The Shift-Left Dividend: Resolving security flaws during architecture saves 10x the engineering cost compared to emergency production patches.

Build security into the fundamental architecture of your models and agents from Day 1. It is the only way to scale safely.
