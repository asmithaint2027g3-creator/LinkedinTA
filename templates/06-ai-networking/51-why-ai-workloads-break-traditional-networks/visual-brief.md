# Visual Brief: Why AI Workloads Break Traditional Networks

## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on why ai workloads break traditional networks, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline
- **Headline**: WHY AI WORKLOADS BREAK NETWORKS
- **Sub-headline**: North-South Web Traffic vs. Synchronized East-West AI Tensor Incast

## 3. Visual Content Hierarchy
- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`AI NETWORKING`) + Topic ID (`#051`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:
  1. The Incast Congestion Storm: In All-Reduce operations, hundreds of GPUs simultaneously blast gigabytes of gradients to the same destination node.
  2. The Buffer Bloat Catastrophe: Legacy switches drop packets when micro-burst buffers overflow, triggering TCP retransmission storms.
  3. Synchronization Barriers: Because distributed training requires all GPUs to reach synchronization points together, a single dropped packet stalls the entire 1,000-GPU cluster.
  4. The Solution: Lossless fabric architectures with Priority Flow Control (PFC) and Explicit Congestion Notification (ECN).
- **Takeaway Rule Banner**: "You cannot run next-generation AI factories on yesterday's web networking architectures."
