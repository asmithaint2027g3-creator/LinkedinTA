CFOs are right to be skeptical of enterprise AI business cases.

In standard SaaS investments, ROI math is predictable: You pay $40/user/month, decommission a legacy tool, and quantify hours saved. In Generative AI, that model breaks down completely.

1. 1. The Variable Compute Trap: Software historically had near-zero marginal cost per transaction. Generative AI has significant marginal cost per token. If token volume scales 4x, cloud bills increase in lockstep unless unit costs are capped.

2. 2. Phantom Productivity: Saving 15 minutes a day across 2,000 employees does not appear on your income statement unless those hours directly reduce outsourced spend or expand revenue capacity.

3. 3. The Long-Tail Validation Drag: A model that is 90% accurate sounds impressive, but in regulated finance or healthcare, humans must review 100% of transactions to catch the 10% errors—frequently doubling cycle time.

4. 4. The Solution: Transitioning from vague 'productivity gains' to hard task unit economics and structured FinOps token budgets.

Measure cost per successful autonomous business outcome, not cost per GPU hour.
