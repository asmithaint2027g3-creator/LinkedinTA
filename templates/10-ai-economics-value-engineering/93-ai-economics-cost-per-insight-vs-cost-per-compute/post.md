3. Factoring in Human Validation Drag: Every inaccurate answer generated by a low-cost model requires expensive human review, completely destroying the theoretical savings.

4. High-Performance Economic Advantage: Finely tuned high-density systems produce accurate insights at a dramatically lower all-in cost per successful transaction.
