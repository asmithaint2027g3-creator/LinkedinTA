If an AI business proposal relies on 'hours saved' to justify a $5M capex investment, reject it.

CFOs are being inundated with AI capital requests that read like marketing brochures rather than disciplined financial models.

1. 1. Hard Cash Impact vs Soft Time Savings: Demanding proof of direct operational cost reduction, contractor spend displacement, or verified incremental revenue.

2. 2. Variable Unit Economics: Modeling how cost scales as transaction volume grows: Does marginal cost per unit decay, or does it balloon linearly?

3. 3. Capex vs Opex Trade-Offs: Comparing multi-year cloud API commitments against sovereign high-density infrastructure ownership.

4. 4. Residual Asset Value: Evaluating whether the investment builds proprietary corporate intellectual property (custom weights, sovereign data graphs) or simply enriches external API vendors.

Evaluate AI through the same uncompromising lens of capital efficiency, unit margins, and balance sheet resilience as any other major corporate investment.
