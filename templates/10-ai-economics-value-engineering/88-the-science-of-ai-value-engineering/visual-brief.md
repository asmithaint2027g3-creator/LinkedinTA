# Visual Brief: The Science of AI Value Engineering

## 1. Visual Purpose
To provide high-authority visual clarity on the science of ai value engineering, illustrating the architectural distinction between unoptimized or legacy enterprise approaches and TrustGrid's verified high-density systems engineering.

## 2. Image Headline & Sub-headline
- **Headline**: THE AI VALUE ENGINEERING METHODOLOGY
- **Sub-headline**: Task Deconstruction, Unit Cost Attribution, and Margin Optimization

## 3. Visual Content Hierarchy
- **Header Plate**: TrustGrid.AI Logo + Category Pill (`AI ECONOMICS & VALUE ENGINEERING`) + Topic ID (`#088`)
- **Core Architecture Cards**:
  1. Deconstructing Cognitive Work: Breaking complex business processes into discrete cognitive tasks (Classification, Extraction, Synthesis, Validation, Decision).
  2. Assigning Unit Task Economics: Calculating the baseline human labor cost per task vs the fully loaded amortized token and infrastructure cost.
  3. Optimizing Model Placement: Routing each task to the most cost-efficient architecture (rules, compact 8B models, fine-tuned 70B models, or frontier reasoning).
  4. Compounding Margin Capture: Tracking operating margin expansion as task straight-through autonomy scales from 50% to 85%+.
- **Takeaway Rule Banner**: "Value engineering turns AI from a speculative technological bet into an engineered financial outcome."
- **Footer**: TrustGrid Engineering Services CTA + trustgrid.ai domain signature
